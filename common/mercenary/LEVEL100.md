# Mercenary level 86–100 content

Migration `utils/sql/mercenary/006_level86_100_content.sql` supplies explicit rows through level 100 for all four roles, both supplied proficiencies and their five tiers. It adds 600 stat rows (40 variants × 15 levels), 188 class/proficiency progression entries, and 40 NPC-template focus-equipment ranges. On the standard restored catalogue the totals become 4,000 stat rows and 625 spell-list rows; the latter includes retired legacy rows. This does not add mercenary types, marketplace services, mercenary AA experience or player-equipped mercenary gear.

## What is reconstructed

The upstream bootstrap still ends stats at 85, and both wizard lists end at 89. Its high-level tail also contains invalid IDs and obsolete bindings. The supplied numeric stats were already emulator approximations: the original contributor explicitly described the level-85 limit in [February/March 2013](https://www.eqemulator.org/forums/archive/index.php/t-35147.html). The [upstream bootstrap](https://github.com/EQEmu/EQEmu/blob/master/utils/sql/merc_tables_bootstrap.sql) is a comparison input, not a missing historical level-100 dataset.

The new numeric rows are **documented emulator reconstruction**, not recovered Live measurements or a claim of exact RoF balance. Per NPC type and numeric field, anchors at levels 75, 80 and 85 define a quadratic continuation. With `d = level − 85`, `s0 = (v80−v75)/5`, `s1 = (v85−v80)/5`, `a = (s1−s0)/10`, the candidate is `v85 + (s1+5a)d + a·d²`. Positive integer fields round down, remain at least their level-85 value and saturate to the column limit; zero/negative sentinels remain unchanged. Neighboring tier curves have a monotonic envelope to prevent quantization from making a higher tier weaker. Journeyman III–V retain the accepted 110/120/130% of matching Journeyman II tuning. HP, mana, AC, ATK, attributes, resists, melee damage, accuracy and regeneration have explicit per-level rows. Attack count/speed/delay, special abilities, run speed and spell/heal scales retain their installed level-85 values. Endurance and skill caps remain derived by the existing class/level/stat implementation.

The repertoire uses existing class-eligible spell/disc definitions, explicit learning levels and actual named Rank I/II IDs. It reconstructs NPC progressions from the supplied role families: tanks receive hate and defensive disciplines; healers receive current remedy/light/renewal/HoT, group-heal, HP/AC/symbol/vie/haste and cure lines; melee mercenaries receive legal targeted stun and damage disciplines; wizards receive updated direct fire/cold damage, lures, stuns, rains, cascades, torrents and hate reduction. Apprentice uses the supplied Rank-I convention and Journeyman Rank-II convention; higher mercenary tiers continue their stat/focus differences. Neither spell Rank III nor a new spell definition is invented.

**Era limit:** the local spell catalogue and current RoF2 ID/name file are not authenticated immutable May 2013 releases. Numeric IDs, class levels and effects are exact to the reviewed input, while exact historical NPC repertoire, frequency, balance and every original release-date association remain unproven. Selected families target the known HoT/VoA/RoF level bands and avoid the later ≥40000 ability cohort, but that numeric cutoff alone is not date proof. This is a playable-era reconstruction candidate, not a historical-parity certification. The current client file's modification date or a `.original` filename must not be represented as period evidence.

## Source behavior and stances

The matching source change fills the previously empty level-86+ melee Nuke-selection branch with targeted physical skill attacks or a usable stun. It checks the loaded stance list, target type/LOS, a positive range, no stealth requirement, endurance and both spell/discipline reuse timers. All 18 selected rogue entries have retained positive ranges of 50 or 200; the SQL binds those values explicitly. Physical damage is preferred; stuns are skipped for immune/already-stunned targets. Existing UseDiscipline performs the cast, resource and reuse handling. Its affordability check now matches native client admission: endurance equal to cost is sufficient, and insufficient endurance is rejected before reuse timers are reserved. Zero-cost disciplines can be used at zero endurance. Lower-level role-selection AI is unchanged; the affordability correction applies to all mercenary disciplines.

Content stance zero means the existing role's allowed combat stances, not permission for Passive to attack: existing Passive suppression remains. Healer Efficient/Reactive decisions, wizard Balanced/Burn/Burn-AE targeting and mana behavior, melee Burn behavior, and tank role restrictions continue through existing AI. NPC-specific historical stance/repertoire differences are not newly asserted. New repertoire rows are scoped to levels 86–100, so accepted level-85-and-below selection remains intact.

The old perpetual taunt, resurrection, Hide, Thief's Eyes and supplied rogue escape utilities remain. Fifty exact known legacy repertoire rows are capped/disabled only above 85; no unrelated/custom row is broadly deleted. New rows replace the high-level slice atomically. Template focus items retain their existing effects and gain explicit 86–100 inventory ranges; these are existing NPC internal focuses, not the later player gear system. Appearance/weapon ranges already cover the new levels. All 20 retained focus spell definitions have no SPA134 LimitMaxLevel or SPA142 LimitMinLevel ceiling; levels 86–100 are not blocked by a focus level cap. Other filters still apply—for example, the cast-time focus requires a cast of at least 3000ms and does not speed the fast Remedy line. Exact item-to-focus and effect definitions are guarded by the SQL. No shared focus definition or item is changed.

## Apply and recovery

Install after the standard catalogue and migration 005. Use the matched source cohort; keep `Mercs:ScaleAboveContentLevel` enabled so installations without the new rows retain their existing fallback. This migration does not change rules, prices, contracts, buffs, characters, currencies, AA ownership or shared spell definitions. It supplies exact-level content so the generic stat and last-repertoire fallback are no longer needed for the installed slice. Missing/uninstalled content still uses the existing fallback; it is not disabled or renamed as complete content.

The SQL is scoped to reviewed preimages. Back up `merc_stats`, `merc_spell_list_entries` and `merc_inventory`; select their co-located catalogue database, use exclusive content maintenance and a stop-on-error/non-reconnecting MariaDB client. All destinations must be InnoDB. The transaction verifies 120 source anchors, exact selected spell identity/semantics, eight class/proficiency lists and the precise legacy row preimages before committing changes. Conflicting custom target stat rows are refused, not overwritten. Identical already-installed rows are accepted. On an error, roll back/disconnect; never force continuation to COMMIT.

Rollback belongs to the operator's exact before/after receipt: restore only reviewed mutations from the three-table backup after checking for intervening content changes. Do not run a generic deletion by level/name which could remove custom or preexisting equal rows. Deploy the matched source/content/cache cohort and boot fresh zones or use the coordinated catalogue reload. A rule reload alone does not refresh cached mercenary lists.

For automatic release packaging, refresh the sanitized content database artifact after qualified application/readback and pin that exact artifact in the same source release cohort. An old database pin will still ship the old mercenary data despite a new source build. Preserve player tables/state and keep private inputs, client assets and QA artifacts out of the release.

## Validation boundary

Prepared coverage shows every one of the 600 type/level cells and all eight role/proficiency lists has an explicit 86–100 entry path; existing appearance/weapon joins cover the rows. These are source/content derivations, not new gameplay acceptance. The prior core lifecycle/payment/ownership/persistence and restored-rank native evidence remains valid only for its recorded builds. This update requires its own coordinated SQL receipt, matched build/deployment and a small level-100 role/ability acceptance before being described as installed or gameplay-complete. No broad repetition of the old core test campaign is required.
