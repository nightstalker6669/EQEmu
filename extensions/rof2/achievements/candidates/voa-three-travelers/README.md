# Three Veil of Alaris traveler admissions — source proposals

These proposals cover The Pillars of Alra Traveler, The Windsong Sanctuary Traveler and The Sepulcher of Order Traveler. Their existing ZoneEnter rules already target730,731and733. No new NPC, quest, opcode, structure, coordinate trigger or engine framework is needed.

Nothing here has been applied or registered. The two SQL files are separate so the normal zone-access policy can be reviewed independently from achievement admission. They guard exact existing identities and policies, reject custom or missing semantics, and change no character progress, optional components, rewards or parent achievements.

The zone proposal changes only min_status80to0 for the three reviewed version0 rows; it preserves expansion ownership, level/content/flag settings and does not grant task or dynamic-zone membership. Version0 data can also serve instances, so mission and raid request/member gates must remain effective. Do not use a GM teleport as ordinary-access acceptance.

The historical fragment has dated2012individual visit/no-reward evidence. It has not been merged into the active historical manifest. Current guide statements about open-world access corroborate the proposal but do not constitute a frozen2013access snapshot. No minimum95 requirement is inferred from a modern webpage header.

Before any deployment, review the access proposal and preserved instance gates, review the completed16-case isolated SQL protocol batch, then schedule normal-player acceptance after the remaining access/cache gates are resolved. Safe points and map/nav files are configuration/assets, not safe-route proof. Stored routine names must be absent before installation; no foreign routine is overwritten. The parent Explorer remains separate because its other seven required children and reward flag still need review.

The bounded source review confirmed nonzero instance entry and dynamic-zone selection still require live membership and matching instance/zone checks, independently of min_status. It did **not** establish working period VoA mission/raid requests: the audited three local quest folders and matching task titles are absent; known General Terasal/Sentry Stone spawns do not establish quest mechanics. See `provenance.json`. No task, member, lockout, NPC or spell table is changed by these proposals.

The two proposals passed16isolated actual MariaDB client cases: three-row access/admission, exact reruns, identity/reward/access/engine refusals, foreign-routine preservation and transaction rollback after a third update failed. The parent, other required children, Alaran definitions and separateversion1gate rows were conserved. No production zone/definition changes were made.
