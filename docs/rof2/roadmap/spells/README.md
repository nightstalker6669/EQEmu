## Scope and counting

- 5,327 existing player-spell IDs have indexed published changes, displayed in 5,629 class memberships. New spell-definition claims: ZERO. Shared spells repeat by class without increasing the unique total; unchanged baseline spells are excluded.
- Change categories overlap and must not be summed. Restored scroll items, acquisition routes and optional implementation support are distinct from baseline spell definitions.
- Published source presence does not establish activation or native purchase/scribe/cast acceptance. Each route retains its own qualification and gates. Original mission references are not automatically newly restored routes.
- The 744 restored scroll-item records do not imply 744 working acquisition routes. Two House of Thule exchange routes remain on hold; refund inputs and withheld choices are excluded from enabled reward lists.
- Optional pet/aura support uses estimates, not authenticated Live balance. NPC-only/helper references and unresolved research candidates are excluded.
- Level means retained class-specific native learning level, not exchange tier/page or a new historical level audit. Ranks remain separate identities.
- Void stock remains77 existing spell IDs. Earned B contributes8, C adds14 (22 cumulative), and Field of Scale adds18 at D (40 cumulative). D costs175 Chronobines; E–G ordinary progression remains held. The earlier Mindfreeze19702/165 proof is retained; one Funnel of Frigidity19688/175 purchase and learn passed with the Field of Scale QA qualification.
- The r25 matching server release is published; native acceptance remains the recorded local Linux scope. Builds/publication do not certify every class, route or platform.
