## Scope and qualifications

- 11,662 distinct added recipe IDs from the accepted owner inventory; physical recipe variants may share an output name. These are not the entire existing EQEmu/PEQ database.
- The 310 accepted batches include 52 with zero additions. Existing recipe corrections and access changes are listed separately; their overlapping counts are not additions.
- Stored skill 75 is retained as No Skill / quest and container combines. Fletcher Ceramic Clay, recipe 3092635, stores skill 62 (Sense Traps) and remains unclassified, not reassigned to Fletching.
- Recorded application/publication evidence does not establish native gameplay acceptance. Eight recharge recipes lack native gameplay proof.
- Five reviewed trivial values, 32 later recipe scalars and two Dwarf folded-sheet quantities use approved LOCAL reconstruction. Basilisk stew is a local restoration with source UNCONFIRMED.
- Three unsupported exact-trivial variants remain unresolved: Fine Xakra Runed Jar (>250), Vale Studded Leggings (106<x≤108) and Chronium Plate (0<x≤188). Chronium remains NEVERLEARN; Vale retains Halfling/kit restrictions.
- Six current 105-tier Distillate XVIII source variants are excluded by scope policy/inference, not proven historical absence. HEHE Meat retains a historical DNC report, not proof it never existed.
- Configured expansion floors are local availability settings, not authenticated historical introduction dates. This finite inventory is not certification of all through-RoF crafting.
